5 Stoic Rules for a Better Life

(and how to start implementing them)

The Stoics lived over 2,000 years ago.

Think about how much the world has changed since then.

Technology. Communication. The pace of life.

And yet, their advice is just as relevant today as it was back then.

Here are 5 rules I try to remind myself of:

1. Wake Up Early - Aurelius
↳ Start your day on your terms before distractions kick in.

→ Set an alarm 30 minutes before your day starts for some quiet reflection.


2. Only Focus On What You Can Control - Epictetus
↳ Worrying about things you can’t change is wasted energy.

→ Next time you’re stressed, ask: “Can I actually change this?” If not, let it go.


3. Don’t Worry About the Future - Seneca
↳ Anxiety comes from trying to solve problems that haven’t happened yet.

→ When you catch yourself spiralling, write down what you can do today, and do it

4. Listen More Than You Speak - Zeno
↳ You learn more when you’re not the one talking.

→ Focus on what others are saying instead of planning your response.


5. Seek Balance In All Things - Posidonius
↳ A good life is about finding harmony between work, rest, and growth.

→ When things feel off, step back and ask: “What’s taking too much of my energy?”


The beauty of Stoicism is in its simplicity: 

small, intentional choices that shape who you become.
